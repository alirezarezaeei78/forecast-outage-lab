Measured MAE on 2,208 UCI Bike Sharing test hours in July–September 2012. Ordinary HGB rises from 31.8 rentals/hour without added reporting delay to 260.7 with a one-hour delay. Outage-aware training rises from 37.9 to 48.9. At three and six hours its MAE is about 51.5, similar to the calendar-only model. Calendar-only and seasonal fallback remain about 51.5 and 56.0. Three expanding chronological folds; simulated delays; one-hour forecast horizon. The training intervention trades undelayed accuracy for resilience in this historical test.
